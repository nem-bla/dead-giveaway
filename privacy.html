Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

This policy covers yt-showrunner, a private application operated by the owner of the Dead Giveaway YouTube channel. It is used only by that owner, with their own Google account.

What the app accesses

Using the YouTube API Services, and only with the account owner's explicit consent through Google's OAuth screen, the app can:

  • upload videos, thumbnails and captions to the owner's channel, and update their metadata and playlists;
  • read the owner's channel and video information, and YouTube Analytics and reporting data for the owner's channel.

The app does not access, collect or store data about any other YouTube user, apart from the aggregated, anonymous statistics YouTube Analytics provides to channel owners.

Storage and sharing

The OAuth refresh token is stored in the owner's macOS Keychain on the owner's own computer. Analytics data is stored locally in the owner's private files. No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Locally stored data can be deleted at any time by the owner.

YouTube and Google

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.
